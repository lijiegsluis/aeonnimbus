02 · Thesis

The market priced one customer's share loss as a verdict on the whole foundry

Fabrinet grew revenue 36% to $4.64 billion in FY26 while Nvidia, its former largest customer, fell about 20%. Data center is 51% of the quarterly mix and Q1 FY27 is guided up 43%. The stock fell 20% the day after that print and closed October 2 at $463.69, 38% below its May high, at 32.9× trailing EPS against 35.2× at its 52-week low a year ago, when growth was half today's rate.

What the market believes
Nvidia's share loss, a 12% gross margin and a 5.25% ten-year yield make FN a supplier. B. Riley (Neutral, $598) and Needham (Buy, $650) both cite the Nvidia loss.
Where we differ
It is a mix question. Ex-Nvidia revenue grew 57%; operating margin hit a three-year high of 10.9%. Our $580 needs no re-rating: 27.5× FY28E economic EPS of $21.11 against 27.7× today.
What we concede
Our own DCF ($276) and the house blend ($394) both sit below the price. The trade is a twelve-month earnings call, so it is sized at 5% and stopped at $337, on the assumption that the DCF is right.

The question is not whether FN is cheap on cash flows. It is whether a 35% grower should trade below the multiple it held at its trough.

03 · Business model

Fabrinet builds what others design, and competes with none of them

Cisco, Nvidia, Nokia and Amazon design optical products; Fabrinet builds them. Its product is manufacturing capability: precision optical packaging, fibre alignment, hermetic sealing, burn-in and test, run at volume on three Thai campuses by 21,521 people. Customers pay a per-unit price that passes most of the bill of materials through, so a 12.2% gross margin says little. The economics sit below the gross line: SG&A is 2.0% of revenue and the non-GAAP operating margin reached 10.9% in Q4.

07 · Three pillars

Diversified demand, cheap capacity and a derating that outran the numbers

01

Diversification: ex-Nvidia revenue grew 57%

Nvidia went from 27.6% of revenue to 16.3%, about $944M to $757M. Everything else went from $2.48B to $3.88B. Cisco grew 48%. Amazon crossed 10% and ramps as a hyperscaler-direct transceiver customer this quarter, and a second merchant transceiver customer starts in the December quarter.

02

Capacity converts to profit at about 11 cents per dollar

Each extra $1B of revenue adds about $110M of operating profit, or $2.57 of economic EPS. Capacity rises from $5.8B to about $9.8B by the March quarter; our FY28E revenue of $7.9B uses 81% of it. The CFO guides FY27 capex near $250M, funded from cash and a $75M term loan.

03

Below 52-week-low multiples with twice the growth

At the October 2025 low FN traded at 3.5× sales and 35.2× trailing EPS while growing 19%. Today: 3.4× and 32.9× while guiding 43%. FN fell 20.3% on August 18 alongside Coherent and AAOI, and 6% when Corning filed a $2B ATM. Neither changed FN's order book.

Against us: FN is not cheap against its five-year median of 26.0× trailing GAAP earnings, and the discount rate has risen and not yet reversed.
