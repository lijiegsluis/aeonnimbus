# Direction: "The Ledger"
Editorial research-house look: a printed ledger for markets. Trust through restraint and visible numbers.
- Mood: quiet, exact, accountable. Paper and ink, one gold accent used only for numbers that matter.
- Fonts: EB Garamond (display + body), IBM Plex Mono (figures, labels).
- Palette: paper #F5F2EC, ink #1A1814, gold #8B6914, rule #D9D2C3, positive green #2F5D3A, dark mode ink #0E0D0B / paper-on-ink.
- Layout: asymmetric 12-col grid, giant tabular figures, hairline rules instead of cards, no purple gradients, no 3-card rows.
- Motion: ease-out, <=240ms, fade/translate-up on scroll reveal, 0.97 press scale, prefers-reduced-motion respected.
- Taste dials: variance 7, motion 3, density 4.
- Source of truth for numbers: live record in /index.html (11 closed calls, 100% hit rate, +34.1% average).
