Approach

The market misprices divergence.

Bottom-up in execution, top-down in framing. Find where consensus embeds a story the business no longer supports.

01 · Edge

Narrative vs. reality

Sector is irrelevant. The gap between the story and the unit economics is the opportunity.

02 · Construction

Asymmetric risk, defined loss

Every call enters with a stop. Maximum loss is known before the position opens.

03 · Process

Catalyst-driven timing

Being right too early is being wrong. Each thesis names the event that forces a repricing.

04 · Accountability

Published before the outcome

Thesis, entry, stop and size go public first. Nothing is revised retrospectively.
