The problem

Most research is graded after the fact, by the author.

Calls get quietly revised, losers disappear and nobody can tell skill from selective memory. Aeon Nimbus fixes the timestamp: the thesis is published first, so the record cannot be edited later.

How it works

  1. Find the divergence

    Start with what the market believes, then test where numbers and narrative disagree.

  2. Publish the full call

    Thesis, entry, stop-loss and size go out on Substack with a timestamp, ahead of the outcome.

  3. Close it on the record

    Every exit is logged, including the weakest. Nothing is deleted.
